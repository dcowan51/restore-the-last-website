  1. 1 / 7

    It starts with a home that's already there.

    We look for places already caring for the last: a children's home, a group home, a campus for young adults who aged out.

  2. 2 / 7

    We name it and publish the cost.

    Every project names the people, the place and the price. Three CalmBoxes for a Phoenix group home: $900.

  3. 3 / 7

    You give to it, or to the fund.

    Pick a project and your gift stays with it. Give to the fund and it goes to whichever home is ready first.

  4. 4 / 7

    Every dollar goes to the field.

    Our founders and board cover the operating costs themselves. Your gift pays for equipment, training and support at the homes.

  5. 5 / 7

    The box arrives. The staff learn it.

    We train the people who already love these kids, so the calm stays after we leave. Their program, their schedule, their people.

  6. 6 / 7

    After a hard visit, there's a way back down.

    Family visits can stir everything up. In that Phoenix home, kids will reach for a CalmBox afterward to find their footing again.

  7. 7 / 7

    And you hear what changed.

    Reports from the field: the sessions, the sleep, the stories. You see where your gift went, and who it reached.

The five steps, in full+

Find. We look for established homes with community trust, staff willing to learn, and leaders committed to outcomes.

Fund. Donors give to a named project or the shared fund. Every project is published, with outcomes attached.

Deploy. The technology goes into the home itself. No referrals. Residents just walk down the hall, so far more of them take part.

Train. On-site staff learn hands-on until they can run sessions with confidence.

Support. We stay: troubleshooting, encouragement, retraining when staff change.

Where every dollar goes+

Our founders and board pay Restore the Last's operating costs out of their own pockets. Donations pay only for the work: equipment, training and support at partner homes.

At checkout you can choose to cover the card processing fee, so your full gift reaches the field. It's optional.
